# Marketing Brief Analyzer

## Purpose

Help digital marketing teams understand and organize client marketing requirements before developing a campaign.

## When to Use

Use this skill when:
- A client submits a new marketing brief.
- A campaign requires initial analysis.
- Marketing requirements need to be summarized.
- The team needs to identify missing campaign information.

## Required Input

- Client or brand name.
- Product or service description.
- Marketing objectives.
- Target audience.
- Campaign duration.
- Marketing platforms.
- Budget, if provided.
- Brand guidelines.
- Expected deliverables.

## Workflow

1. Review the client's marketing brief.
2. Identify the main campaign objective.
3. Extract the target audience information.
4. Identify the product's main selling points.
5. Determine the requested marketing platforms.
6. Organize campaign requirements.
7. Identify missing or unclear information.
8. Prepare a structured brief summary.

## Output Format

# Marketing Brief Summary

**Client / Brand:**  

**Product / Service:**  

**Campaign Objective:**  

**Target Audience:**  

**Main Message:**  

**Marketing Platforms:**  

**Campaign Duration:**  

**Expected Deliverables:**  

**Missing Information:**  

## Rules

- Do not invent client requirements.
- Do not assume a marketing budget.
- Preserve the client's original objectives.
- Clearly identify missing information.
- Do not guarantee campaign success.

## Example Request

"Analyze this client marketing brief and summarize the main objectives, target audience, platforms, and required deliverables."

## Expected Result

A structured marketing brief summary ready for review by the marketing team.
