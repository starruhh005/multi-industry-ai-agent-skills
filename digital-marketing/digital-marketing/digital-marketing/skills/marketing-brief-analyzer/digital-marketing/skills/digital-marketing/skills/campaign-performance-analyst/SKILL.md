# Campaign Performance Analyst

## Purpose

Help digital marketing teams review campaign results and identify performance patterns based on available data.

## When to Use

Use this skill when:
- A social media campaign has finished.
- Advertising performance needs to be evaluated.
- Engagement metrics need to be summarized.
- Marketers need to compare campaign results.
- A client requests a campaign performance review.

## Required Input

- Campaign name.
- Campaign period.
- Platform used.
- Reach.
- Impressions.
- Engagement.
- Clicks.
- Conversions, if available.
- Advertising expenditure, if available.
- Campaign objectives.

## Workflow

1. Review the campaign data.
2. Identify the available performance metrics.
3. Check data completeness.
4. Calculate relevant metrics when the necessary data is available.
5. Compare results with campaign objectives or previous periods when comparable data exists.
6. Identify performance patterns.
7. Prepare findings and recommendations.
8. Present the analysis for marketer verification.

## Common Metrics

- Reach.
- Impressions.
- Engagement rate.
- Click-through rate (CTR).
- Conversion rate.
- Cost per click (CPC).
- Cost per acquisition (CPA).

Only calculate metrics when the required data is available and the calculation method is appropriate.

## Rules

- Never fabricate campaign data.
- Do not calculate metrics using missing information.
- Explain the calculation method when relevant.
- Distinguish actual results from estimates.
- Do not guarantee future campaign performance.
- Require marketer verification before presenting final conclusions to clients.
