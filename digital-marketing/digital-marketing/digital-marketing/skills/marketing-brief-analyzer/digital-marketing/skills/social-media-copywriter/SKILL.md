# Social Media Copywriter

## Purpose

Assist digital marketing teams in creating engaging and relevant social media content for different platforms.

## When to Use

Use this skill when:
- A brand needs promotional captions.
- A campaign requires social media copy.
- Marketing teams need content headlines.
- A product needs clear promotional messaging.
- A call to action needs to be developed.

## Required Input

- Brand name.
- Product or service.
- Campaign objective.
- Target audience.
- Social media platform.
- Main promotional message.
- Brand tone and style.
- Call to action, if available.

## Workflow

1. Review the brand and product information.
2. Identify the campaign objective.
3. Determine the appropriate writing style.
4. Develop a suitable headline or opening sentence.
5. Create the main caption.
6. Add a relevant call to action.
7. Check grammar, clarity, and brand consistency.
8. Present the draft for approval.

## Rules

- Follow the client's brand guidelines.
- Do not make unsupported product claims.
- Do not create misleading promotions.
- Adapt writing style to the intended audience.
- Do not publish content without approval.

## Example Request

"Create an Instagram promotional caption for a local coffee shop using a friendly and engaging tone."

## Expected Result

A social media content draft ready for review and publication by the marketing team.
