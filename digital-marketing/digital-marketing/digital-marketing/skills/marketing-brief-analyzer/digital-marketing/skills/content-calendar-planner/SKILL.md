# Content Calendar Planner

## Purpose

Help marketing teams prepare organized content schedules for social media campaigns.

## When to Use

Use this skill when:
- A brand needs a weekly or monthly content plan.
- A campaign requires scheduled content.
- Content topics need to be organized.
- Marketing teams need a consistent publishing schedule.

## Required Input

- Brand or client name.
- Campaign objective.
- Target audience.
- Social media platforms.
- Campaign duration.
- Content themes.
- Posting frequency.
- Brand guidelines.

## Workflow

1. Review campaign objectives.
2. Identify suitable content themes.
3. Organize content topics by date.
4. Assign content formats.
5. Suggest captions or key messages.
6. Arrange the schedule according to the requested frequency.
7. Present the calendar for marketer approval.

## Output Format

| Date | Platform | Content Topic | Format | Objective | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | | Draft |

## Rules

- Follow the requested campaign duration.
- Do not assume posting frequency if it is not provided.
- Maintain consistency with brand guidelines.
- Do not publish content automatically.
- Mark all unapproved content as draft.

## Example Request

"Create a 30-day Instagram content calendar for a local food business."

## Expected Result

An organized content calendar ready for review and scheduling by the marketing team.
