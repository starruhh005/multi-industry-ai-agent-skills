# Audience Persona Builder

## Purpose

Help marketing teams organize audience characteristics into useful customer personas.

## When to Use

Use this skill when:
- A campaign requires target audience identification.
- Customer research needs to be organized.
- A brand needs a clearer audience profile.
- Marketing messages need to be adapted to customer segments.

## Required Input

- Product or service description.
- Customer research data.
- Age range, if available.
- Location information, if available.
- Customer interests.
- Customer needs and challenges.
- Purchasing behavior, if supported by data.

## Workflow

1. Review the available audience research.
2. Identify common customer characteristics.
3. Group audiences into relevant segments.
4. Identify customer needs and interests.
5. Develop a representative persona.
6. Explain the basis of the persona.
7. Mark assumptions that require further research.

## Rules

- Do not present assumptions as verified facts.
- Do not invent survey results.
- Base personas on available research.
- Clearly label illustrative personas.
- Avoid unnecessary sensitive personal information.

## Example Request

"Create a target audience persona for this product based on the customer research provided."

## Expected Result

A structured customer persona that can support campaign planning and content development.
