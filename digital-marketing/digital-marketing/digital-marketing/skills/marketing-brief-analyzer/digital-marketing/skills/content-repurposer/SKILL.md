# Content Repurposer

## Purpose

Help marketing teams reuse existing content by adapting it into different formats and social media platforms.

## When to Use

Use this skill when:
- Existing content needs to be adapted for another platform.
- A long article needs to become shorter social media content.
- A video script needs to become a caption.
- Marketing teams want to reuse campaign materials.
- One promotional message needs multiple content formats.

## Required Input

- Original content.
- Original content format.
- Target platform.
- Desired content format.
- Target audience.
- Brand tone and style.
- Main message that must be preserved.

## Workflow

1. Review the original content.
2. Identify its main message and important information.
3. Determine the target platform and format.
4. Adapt the structure and writing style.
5. Preserve important facts and original meaning.
6. Check whether the adapted content follows platform requirements.
7. Present the result for marketer review.

## Rules

- Do not change the original meaning.
- Do not add unsupported information.
- Preserve important product and campaign details.
- Follow the brand's communication guidelines.
- Do not publish without approval.

## Example Request

"Convert this promotional article into an Instagram caption and a short TikTok video script."

## Expected Result

Platform-specific content drafts that retain the original marketing message.
