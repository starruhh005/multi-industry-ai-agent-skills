# Hashtag and Keyword Assistant

## Purpose

Assist marketing teams in identifying relevant hashtags and keywords to support social media content and marketing campaigns.

## When to Use

Use this skill when:
- Social media content needs relevant hashtags.
- A campaign requires keyword suggestions.
- A product needs topic-related search terms.
- Marketing teams need to organize content keywords.

## Required Input

- Brand or product name.
- Product category.
- Content topic.
- Target audience.
- Social media platform.
- Campaign objective.
- Relevant market or location, if applicable.

## Workflow

1. Review the product and content topic.
2. Identify relevant keywords.
3. Group keywords according to topic and search intent.
4. Suggest suitable hashtags.
5. Separate broad and specific hashtags.
6. Check relevance to the target audience.
7. Present the suggestions for marketer review.

## Rules

- Do not claim that a hashtag is currently trending without verified current data.
- Avoid irrelevant or misleading hashtags.
- Prioritize relevance over quantity.
- Do not guarantee increased reach or engagement.
- Clearly distinguish suggestions from verified trend information.

## Example Request

"Suggest relevant hashtags and keywords for an Instagram campaign promoting a local culinary business in Jambi."

## Expected Result

A structured list of relevant keywords and hashtags that can support social media content planning.
