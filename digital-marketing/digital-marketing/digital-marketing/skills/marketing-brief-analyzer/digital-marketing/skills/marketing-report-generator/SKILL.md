# Marketing Report Generator

## Purpose

Assist digital marketing teams in preparing periodic campaign reports and client performance summaries.

## When to Use

Use this skill when:
- A client requests a monthly marketing report.
- A campaign has completed.
- Marketing activities need to be documented.
- Campaign performance findings need to be presented.
- Teams need to prepare recommendations for the next period.

## Required Input

- Client or brand name.
- Reporting period.
- Campaign objectives.
- Marketing activities.
- Platforms used.
- Campaign performance data.
- Key findings.
- Challenges or obstacles.
- Recommendations, if available.

## Workflow

1. Review the reporting period and campaign objectives.
2. Organize the marketing activities.
3. Summarize the available campaign metrics.
4. Identify key performance findings.
5. Describe reported challenges.
6. Prepare recommendations based on the available evidence.
7. Generate a structured report draft.
8. Present the report for marketer review and client approval.

## Output Format

# Digital Marketing Performance Report

## 1. Client and Campaign Information

- Client / Brand:
- Campaign Name:
- Reporting Period:

## 2. Campaign Objectives

Describe the intended marketing objectives.

## 3. Marketing Activities

Summarize the activities carried out during the reporting period.

## 4. Performance Results

Present available campaign metrics and relevant comparisons.

## 5. Key Findings

Explain the main findings based on the available data.

## 6. Challenges and Obstacles

Describe reported challenges during campaign implementation.

## 7. Recommendations

Provide practical recommendations supported by campaign findings.

## 8. Conclusion

Summarize the overall campaign performance.

## Rules

- Use only verified campaign information.
- Do not invent statistics or achievements.
- Distinguish actual results from estimates.
- Do not make unsupported performance claims.
- Follow the client's reporting format when provided.
- Mark the report as a draft until approved.

## Example Request

"Prepare a monthly digital marketing report for this client using the campaign data and activity records provided."

## Expected Result

A structured marketing report draft ready for review and presentation to the client.
