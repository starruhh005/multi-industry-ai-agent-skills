# Jobdesk Analysis — Digital Marketing Agency

## 1. Overview

This document identifies the main responsibilities and repetitive activities of employees working in a digital marketing agency.

The objective is to determine which activities can be supported by an AI Agent to improve marketing team productivity and operational efficiency.

## 2. Jobdesk and Repetitive Activities

| No. | Jobdesk | Repetitive Activity | AI Agent Support |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Marketing research | Collecting and summarizing product and competitor information | Organizes available research information |
| 2 | Audience research | Identifying customer characteristics and interests | Develops target audience personas |
| 3 | Content planning | Preparing weekly and monthly content schedules | Generates structured content calendars |
| 4 | Copywriting | Writing captions, promotional text, and headlines | Creates content drafts based on brand guidelines |
| 5 | Social media management | Adapting content for different platforms | Repurposes content into platform-specific formats |
| 6 | Hashtag and keyword research | Finding relevant hashtags and keywords | Suggests relevant keywords based on available information |
| 7 | Campaign monitoring | Reviewing advertising and social media metrics | Summarizes and analyzes campaign data |
| 8 | Marketing reporting | Preparing campaign performance reports | Generates structured marketing report drafts |

## 3. Tasks Suitable for AI Assistance

- Analyzing marketing briefs.
- Organizing audience research.
- Creating content calendars.
- Drafting social media captions.
- Adapting content for different platforms.
- Suggesting hashtags and keywords.
- Calculating and summarizing campaign metrics.
- Preparing marketing reports.

## 4. Tasks Requiring Human Supervision

The following activities must remain under the supervision of marketing professionals:

- Final marketing strategy decisions.
- Approval of promotional claims.
- Publication of social media content.
- Advertising budget decisions.
- Client negotiations.
- Final approval of campaign reports.

## 5. Expected Benefits

1. Faster content production.
2. More organized marketing schedules.
3. Consistent brand communication.
4. Reduced repetitive administrative work.
5. More efficient campaign reporting.
6. Better use of available marketing data.

## 6. Conclusion

AI Agents can support digital marketing agencies by assisting with research, audience analysis, content planning, copywriting, campaign monitoring, and reporting.

However, marketers remain responsible for strategic decisions, content approval, client relationships, and the final evaluation of campaign results.
