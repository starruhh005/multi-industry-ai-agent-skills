# Anamnesis Interview Assistant

## Purpose

Assist clinic staff in documenting the patient's initial complaints and relevant information through a structured question-and-answer process.

## When to Use

Use this skill when:
- A patient needs to provide initial complaint information.
- Staff need to organize anamnesis responses.
- Initial patient information needs to be prepared for medical personnel.

## Required Input

- Patient's main complaint.
- When the complaint started.
- Duration of the complaint.
- Symptoms described by the patient.
- Relevant medical history, if requested by authorized staff.
- Allergies, if relevant and provided.
- Other information required by the clinic's approved form.

## Workflow

1. Ask the patient to describe their main complaint in their own words.
2. Record when the complaint started.
3. Ask relevant follow-up questions based on the clinic's approved questionnaire.
4. Record the patient's answers accurately.
5. Identify unanswered questions.
6. Organize the information into a structured anamnesis note.
7. Present the note to qualified medical personnel.

## Rules

- Preserve the patient's original meaning.
- Do not invent symptoms or medical history.
- Do not diagnose a disease.
- Do not recommend medication or treatment.
- Do not replace professional medical assessment.
- Immediately refer urgent or emergency complaints to clinic staff according to clinic procedures.
