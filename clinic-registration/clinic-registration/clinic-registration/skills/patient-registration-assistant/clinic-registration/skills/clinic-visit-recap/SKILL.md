# Clinic Visit Recap

## Purpose

Assist clinic staff in preparing structured administrative summaries of patient visits.

## When to Use

Use this skill when:
- A patient visit record needs to be organized.
- Registration information and initial complaints need to be combined.
- Staff need a concise visit recap for administrative documentation.

## Required Input

- Patient identifier.
- Visit date.
- Registration information.
- Initial complaint.
- Initial anamnesis notes.
- Administrative status of the visit.
- Other approved information.

## Workflow

1. Collect the available visit information.
2. Organize registration details.
3. Summarize the initial complaint.
4. Include anamnesis information provided by the patient.
5. Identify missing administrative fields.
6. Prepare a structured visit recap.
7. Present the result for staff verification.

## Output Format

# Clinic Visit Administrative Recap

**Patient Identifier:**  
**Visit Date:**  
**Registration Status:**  

## Registration Information

Summarize relevant administrative information.

## Initial Complaint

Summarize the patient's reported complaint.

## Initial Anamnesis

Present the information recorded during the initial interview.

## Administrative Notes

List missing information or other administrative notes.

## Verification Status

Pending Staff Review / Verified by Authorized Staff

## Rules

- Do not invent visit details.
- Do not create a diagnosis or treatment plan.
- Do not change medical records without authorization.
- Keep patient information confidential.
- Use synthetic data for public demonstrations.
- Ensure authorized staff verify the recap.

## Example Request

"Prepare an administrative recap of this dummy patient's clinic visit using the registration and initial anamnesis notes."

## Expected Result

A structured administrative visit recap ready for review by authorized clinic staff.
