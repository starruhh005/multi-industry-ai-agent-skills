# Jobdesk Analysis — Clinic Registration and Anamnesis

## 1. Overview

This document identifies the responsibilities and repetitive activities of staff working in the registration and initial anamnesis section of a clinic.

The purpose is to identify administrative tasks that can be assisted by an AI Agent while ensuring that medical decisions remain under the authority of qualified healthcare professionals.

## 2. Jobdesk and Repetitive Activities

| No. | Jobdesk | Repetitive Activity | AI Agent Support |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Patient registration | Recording patient identity and visit information | Organizes patient registration information |
| 2 | Registration data verification | Checking whether required fields are complete | Identifies missing or inconsistent administrative data |
| 3 | Initial anamnesis | Asking and recording initial patient complaints | Organizes structured questions and patient responses |
| 4 | Patient complaint documentation | Rewriting long complaints into structured notes | Summarizes complaints while preserving their meaning |
| 5 | Patient visit administration | Preparing visit records and summaries | Generates structured administrative visit recaps |
| 6 | Clinic information services | Answering routine questions about schedules and requirements | Provides answers based on verified clinic information |

## 3. Tasks Suitable for AI Assistance

- Recording patient registration information.
- Checking administrative data completeness.
- Organizing initial anamnesis questions and answers.
- Summarizing patient complaints.
- Preparing administrative visit recaps.
- Answering routine questions using official clinic information.

## 4. Tasks Requiring Medical Supervision

The following activities must not be performed independently by the AI Agent:

- Diagnosing a patient's illness.
- Prescribing medication.
- Determining medical treatment.
- Making final clinical triage decisions.
- Interpreting medical examinations as a final diagnosis.
- Making decisions regarding patient care.

## 5. Expected Benefits

1. Faster patient registration.
2. More complete administrative records.
3. Better organization of initial patient information.
4. Reduced repetitive data entry.
5. Easier transfer of structured information to medical staff.

## 6. Conclusion

AI Agents can assist clinic registration staff by organizing patient data, checking administrative completeness, documenting initial complaints, and preparing visit summaries.

However, all medical assessments and clinical decisions must remain under the responsibility of qualified healthcare professionals.
