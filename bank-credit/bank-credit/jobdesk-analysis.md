# Jobdesk Analysis — Bank Credit Administration

## 1. Department Overview

The Credit Administration Department supports the administrative process of credit applications in a bank. Its activities may include receiving applications, organizing supporting documents, checking document completeness, recording applicant information, preparing summaries, monitoring application status, and compiling administrative reports.

Many of these activities are repetitive and require structured data handling. AI Agents can assist with these tasks to reduce manual workload and improve document processing consistency.

## 2. Jobdesk and Repetitive Activities

| No. | Credit Administration Jobdesk | Repetitive Activity | Potential AI Agent Support |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Credit application intake | Recording applicant details and application information | Register and organize application data |
| 2 | Document checking | Checking whether required documents are submitted | Generate and check document completeness lists |
| 3 | Data extraction | Re-entering applicant information from documents into administrative records | Extract and structure information from provided documents |
| 4 | Applicant profile preparation | Summarizing applicant identity, occupation, and financial information | Generate structured applicant profiles |
| 5 | Document verification support | Comparing data across application documents | Identify possible inconsistencies for human verification |
| 6 | Missing document communication | Informing applicants about incomplete requirements | Draft missing-document notifications |
| 7 | Application status administration | Updating and recapping application processing status | Organize status records and prepare progress summaries |
| 8 | Credit administration reporting | Compiling application data and processing statistics | Generate administrative reports and summaries |

## 3. Proposed AI Agent Skills

1. `credit-application-intake` — records and organizes incoming credit applications.
2. `credit-document-checklist` — checks application document completeness against provided requirements.
3. `credit-document-data-extractor` — extracts information from submitted credit documents.
4. `applicant-profile-summarizer` — prepares structured applicant information summaries.
5. `document-consistency-checker` — identifies potential inconsistencies across supporting documents.
6. `missing-document-notifier` — drafts notifications for missing or incomplete documents.
7. `credit-application-status-tracker` — organizes application progress and status information.
8. `credit-administration-report` — prepares credit administration reports.

## 4. Expected Benefits

- Reduces repetitive data entry.
- Speeds up administrative document checks.
- Makes application records more organized.
- Helps identify incomplete application requirements.
- Reduces repeated manual summarization.
- Improves the consistency of administrative reports.
- Allows bank staff to focus on analysis, customer communication, risk assessment, and tasks requiring professional judgment.

## 5. Important Limitations

CreditMate is designed for administrative assistance only. It does not replace banking personnel, credit analysts, or authorized decision-makers.

Credit approval, rejection, credit scoring, loan limits, financial risk judgments, and fraud determinations must remain subject to the bank's official procedures and authorized human review.

All public repository examples and testing data must be synthetic. Real customer documents, account details, identity numbers, financial records, and confidential banking information must not be uploaded to a public GitHub repository.
