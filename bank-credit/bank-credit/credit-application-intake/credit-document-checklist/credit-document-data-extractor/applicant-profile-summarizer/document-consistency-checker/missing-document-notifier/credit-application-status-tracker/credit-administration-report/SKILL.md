# Credit Administration Report

## Purpose

Assist credit administration staff in compiling credit application information into structured administrative reports for routine monitoring, internal coordination, and reporting.

## When to Use

Use this skill when:
- Preparing weekly or monthly credit administration reports.
- Summarizing incoming credit applications.
- Reporting document completeness.
- Summarizing application processing statuses.
- Compiling pending administrative tasks.
- Preparing internal credit administration recaps.

## Required Input

When available, use:
- Reporting period.
- Credit application records.
- Credit product categories.
- Application document checklist results.
- Application status data.
- Pending administrative follow-up records.
- Previous reporting period data.
- Bank reporting template.

## Procedure

1. Identify the reporting period.
2. Review the available application records.
3. Check data completeness and identify inconsistencies.
4. Organize the records by credit product or administrative status.
5. Calculate totals using the available data.
6. Compare reporting periods when previous data is provided.
7. Identify pending applications and administrative follow-up tasks.
8. Summarize key findings without making credit risk judgments.
9. Prepare the report using the provided template or standard structure.
10. Submit the report to authorized bank personnel for review.

## Suggested Report Structure

1. Report title.
2. Reporting period.
3. Executive summary.
4. Number of applications received.
5. Application breakdown by credit product.
6. Document completeness summary.
7. Application status recap.
8. Pending administrative follow-ups.
9. Data limitations and verification notes.
10. Conclusion and administrative recommendations.

## Output Format

The report should contain:
- Clear headings and sections.
- Tables for application counts and statuses.
- Summary of missing or incomplete documents.
- Pending application recap.
- Follow-up actions.
- Data sources and reporting assumptions.
- Notes on records requiring verification.

## Rules and Limitations

- Never fabricate application numbers or statistics.
- Calculate totals and percentages only when data is sufficient.
- Clearly identify missing or incomplete data.
- Do not generate credit approval or rejection recommendations.
- Do not provide official credit scores or credit risk decisions.
- Do not make fraud determinations.
- Protect applicant and customer confidentiality.
- Use synthetic data in public repository demonstrations and testing.
