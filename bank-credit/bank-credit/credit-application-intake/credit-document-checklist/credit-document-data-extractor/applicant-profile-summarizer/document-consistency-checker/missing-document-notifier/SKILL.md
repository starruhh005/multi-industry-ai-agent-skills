# Missing Document Notifier

## Purpose

Assist credit administration staff in preparing standardized notifications for applicants whose credit application documents are missing, incomplete, unreadable, or require clarification.

## When to Use

Use this skill when:
- Required application documents have not been submitted.
- Submitted documents are incomplete.
- Documents are unreadable.
- Additional information is required for administrative processing.
- HR or credit administration staff need a standardized applicant notification draft.

## Required Input

When available, use:
- Applicant name or reference number.
- Credit application type.
- List of required documents.
- List of documents received.
- Missing or incomplete document details.
- Submission instructions.
- Contact or submission channel.
- Bank-approved notification template.

## Procedure

1. Review the credit application checklist.
2. Identify missing or incomplete documents.
3. Confirm that the requirements are based on the provided bank checklist.
4. Organize the missing document list.
5. Identify any submission instructions or deadlines provided by bank staff.
6. Draft a clear and professional notification.
7. Include instructions for submitting the missing documents.
8. Present the notification to authorized staff for review before sending.

### Draft Message

Prepare a polite, professional, and easy-to-understand message that includes:

- Greeting.
- Application reference, if appropriate.
- Explanation of missing or incomplete requirements.
- List of documents or information required.
- Submission instructions.
- Deadline, only if officially provided.
- Closing statement.

## Rules and Limitations

- Do not invent missing document requirements.
- Do not create deadlines without official instructions.
- Do not promise credit approval or processing outcomes.
- Do not send notifications without authorization.
- Protect applicant personal and financial information.
- Use fictional applicant information in public examples.
