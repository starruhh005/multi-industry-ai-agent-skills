# Applicant Profile Summarizer

## Purpose

Assist credit administration staff in compiling applicant information into a structured profile to support document review and administrative preparation.

## When to Use

Use this skill when:
- Preparing applicant profile summaries.
- Compiling information from multiple application documents.
- Organizing applicant employment or business information.
- Preparing an administrative overview for credit staff.

## Required Input

When available, use:
- Applicant identification information.
- Employment or business information.
- Income information.
- Credit application details.
- Supporting document contents.
- Bank-approved summary template.

## Procedure

1. Review the information provided for the applicant.
2. Identify relevant applicant profile categories.
3. Extract information from the available documents.
4. Organize the information into a structured summary.
5. Identify missing or inconsistent fields.
6. Clearly distinguish reported information from verified information.
7. Prepare the profile summary for authorized bank staff.

## Output Format

### Applicant Administrative Profile

- Applicant reference:
- Application type:
- Occupation or business:
- Employment or business details:
- Income information provided:
- Requested credit amount:
- Application purpose:
- Supporting documents:
- Missing information:
- Items requiring verification:

### Summary

Provide a concise summary based only on information available in the supplied documents.

## Rules and Limitations

- Do not invent applicant information.
- Do not interpret the summary as a creditworthiness assessment.
- Do not make approval or rejection recommendations.
- Do not infer fraud or financial risk from incomplete information.
- Do not treat unverified information as confirmed fact.
- Protect confidential banking and customer information.
- Use fictional applicant data in public demonstrations.
