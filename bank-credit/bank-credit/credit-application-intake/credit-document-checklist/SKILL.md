# Credit Document Checklist

## Purpose

Assist credit administration staff in checking whether credit application documents meet the administrative requirements specified by the bank.

## When to Use

Use this skill when:
- Reviewing submitted credit application documents.
- Checking document completeness.
- Preparing a document checklist.
- Identifying missing or incomplete requirements.

## Required Input

When available, use:
- Credit product type.
- Bank document requirements.
- Applicant category.
- Submitted document list.
- Document files or readable document contents.
- Relevant administrative instructions.

## Procedure

1. Identify the credit product and applicant category.
2. Review the official document requirements supplied by the bank.
3. Create a checklist of required documents.
4. Compare the required documents with those submitted.
5. Identify missing, unreadable, incomplete, or expired documents when validity rules are provided.
6. Mark each document's administrative status.
7. Prepare a completeness summary.
8. Forward the result for review by authorized credit administration staff.

## Output Format

| Document | Requirement | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Document name | Required / Optional | Received / Missing / Incomplete / Needs Review | Explanation |

Include a summary of:
- Total required documents.
- Documents received.
- Missing documents.
- Incomplete documents.
- Items requiring manual verification.

## Rules and Limitations

- Use the bank's provided checklist.
- Do not assume all credit products have the same requirements.
- Do not declare a document authentic based only on its presence.
- Do not invent document contents.
- Do not make credit approval or rejection decisions.
- Use fictional documents for public demonstrations and testing.
