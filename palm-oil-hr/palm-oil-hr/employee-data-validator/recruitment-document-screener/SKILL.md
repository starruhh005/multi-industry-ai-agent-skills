# Recruitment Document Screener

## Purpose

Assist HR staff in checking the completeness of recruitment documents and organizing applicant information for administrative review.

## When to Use

Use this skill when:
- Receiving job applications.
- Checking applicant document requirements.
- Organizing CVs and supporting documents.
- Preparing recruitment administration summaries.

## Required Input

When available, use:
- Job vacancy or position.
- Recruitment document checklist.
- Applicant CV or resume.
- Application letter.
- Educational certificates or other required supporting documents.
- Relevant work experience information.

## Procedure

1. Identify the position being applied for.
2. Review the document requirements provided by HR.
3. Check which required documents have been submitted.
4. Identify missing, unreadable, or incomplete documents.
5. Extract relevant information from the applicant's CV.
6. Organize the information into a structured summary.
7. Prepare an administrative screening report for HR staff.

## Output Format

Provide:
- Applicant reference or name, when authorized.
- Applied position.
- Submitted documents.
- Missing or incomplete documents.
- Summary of education and work experience.
- Administrative notes.
- Items requiring HR verification.

## Rules and Limitations

- Do not make final hiring or rejection decisions.
- Do not rank applicants based on unsupported assumptions.
- Do not invent qualifications or work experience.
- Do not infer personal characteristics unrelated to the job requirements.
- Apply only the criteria provided by authorized HR staff.
- Protect applicant personal information.
- Use fictional applicant data in public examples.
