# HR Monthly Report Generator

## Purpose

Assist HR staff in compiling monthly administrative information into structured reports to reduce repetitive data collection, summarization, and report formatting.

## When to Use

Use this skill when:
- Preparing monthly HR reports.
- Summarizing employee headcount information.
- Reporting attendance statistics.
- Summarizing recruitment activities.
- Compiling leave and permission records.
- Summarizing training and employee administration activities.

## Required Input

When available, use:
- Reporting month and year.
- Employee data summary.
- Attendance records.
- Recruitment activity records.
- Leave and permission summaries.
- Training activity data.
- Previous report format.
- Reporting instructions and definitions.

## Procedure

1. Identify the reporting period.
2. Review the available HR datasets.
3. Check data completeness and identify potential inconsistencies.
4. Organize the information by reporting category.
5. Calculate totals and percentages only when sufficient data is provided.
6. Compare current-period figures with previous periods when data is available.
7. Identify important changes, trends, and administrative issues.
8. Distinguish factual findings from observations or recommendations.
9. Prepare a structured monthly HR report.
10. Present the report for HR review before official submission.

## Suggested Report Structure

1. Report title and period.
2. Executive summary.
3. Employee overview.
4. Attendance recap.
5. Recruitment administration.
6. Leave and permission summary.
7. Training and development activities.
8. Administrative issues and follow-up.
9. Conclusion and recommendations.

## Output Format

The report should contain:
- Clear headings.
- Concise explanations.
- Tables for numerical data when appropriate.
- Source or data references.
- Notes on missing data.
- Follow-up items.
- Recommendations based only on the available information.

## Rules and Limitations

- Never fabricate employee numbers, attendance figures, or recruitment statistics.
- Do not calculate percentages using incomplete or unsuitable data.
- Clearly disclose missing data and calculation assumptions.
- Do not make employment or disciplinary decisions.
- Protect confidential employee information.
- Use synthetic HR data in public examples and repository demonstrations.
