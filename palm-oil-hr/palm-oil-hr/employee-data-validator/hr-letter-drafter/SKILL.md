# HR Letter Drafter

## Purpose

Assist HR staff in preparing routine Human Resources administrative letters using standardized templates and information supplied by authorized personnel.

## When to Use

Use this skill when:
- Drafting employment-related administrative letters.
- Preparing assignment or placement letters.
- Drafting employee status confirmation letters.
- Preparing internal HR notifications.
- Creating routine personnel correspondence.

## Required Input

When available, use:
- Letter type.
- Employee name or authorized reference.
- Employee position.
- Department or work unit.
- Relevant dates.
- Letter purpose.
- Approved letter template.
- Authorized instructions.
- Signatory information, if provided.

## Procedure

1. Identify the requested letter type.
2. Review the relevant company template.
3. Collect the information required for the letter.
4. Check for missing or inconsistent information.
5. Organize the information according to the template.
6. Draft the letter in formal and professional language.
7. Mark missing details with placeholders rather than inventing information.
8. Present the draft to HR staff for review and approval.

## Output Format

Prepare a letter draft containing, where applicable:
- Letter number placeholder.
- Date.
- Subject.
- Recipient.
- Employee information.
- Main content.
- Relevant administrative details.
- Signature section for the authorized official.

## Rules and Limitations

- Do not invent letter numbers or authorization details.
- Do not create fake signatures.
- Do not issue or send official letters without authorization.
- Do not make employment decisions on behalf of HR.
- Use only approved templates when provided.
- Maintain confidentiality of employee information.
- Use fictional employee information in public examples.
