# Employee Data Validator

## Purpose

Assist HR staff in checking employee administrative data to identify missing fields, inconsistent entries, duplicate records, and formatting problems.

## When to Use

Use this skill when:
- Reviewing newly entered employee information.
- Checking employee data before preparing an HR report.
- Reviewing employee records for missing information.
- Comparing employee data from multiple administrative sources.

## Required Input

When available, use:
- Employee ID or employee code.
- Employee name.
- Department or work unit.
- Job position.
- Employment status.
- Joining date.
- Contact information.
- Other fields required by the company's HR data standards.

Do not request unnecessary personal information.

## Procedure

1. Review the employee data provided by authorized HR staff.
2. Check whether required fields are present.
3. Identify inconsistent formats, such as date or employee-code formats.
4. Check for possible duplicate employee IDs or records.
5. Compare entries only when supporting reference data is provided.
6. Separate confirmed errors from items that merely require verification.
7. Prepare a structured validation summary.

## Output Format

Provide:
- Total records reviewed.
- Records with complete information.
- Records with missing information.
- Possible duplicate records.
- Inconsistent entries.
- Recommended corrections or follow-up actions.

## Rules and Limitations

- Never invent missing employee information.
- Do not assume that two similar names represent the same employee.
- Do not change official employee records.
- Do not expose employee information to unauthorized parties.
- Mark uncertain findings as requiring HR verification.
- Use synthetic employee data in public examples and demonstrations.
