# Leave and Permission Assistant

## Purpose

Assist HR staff with repetitive leave and permission administration by organizing requests, checking required information, and preparing summaries for authorized personnel.

## When to Use

Use this skill when:
- Recording employee leave requests.
- Processing permission request information.
- Reviewing leave request completeness.
- Preparing leave recapitulation.
- Tracking leave request administrative status.

## Required Input

When available, use:
- Employee ID or authorized employee reference.
- Employee name, if required.
- Leave or permission type.
- Requested date or period.
- Reason provided by the employee.
- Submission date.
- Supporting documents, if required by company policy.
- Applicable company leave policy.

## Procedure

1. Review the leave or permission request.
2. Check whether the required information is complete.
3. Identify the leave or permission category.
4. Organize the requested date or period.
5. Review the request against the provided administrative requirements.
6. Identify missing information or supporting documents.
7. Prepare a leave or permission record.
8. Forward the request or summary to the authorized HR officer for approval.

## Output Format

Provide:
- Employee reference.
- Request type.
- Requested date or period.
- Submission date.
- Required documents status.
- Administrative completeness.
- Current approval status, if supplied.
- Follow-up action required.

## Rules and Limitations

- Do not approve or reject leave requests independently.
- Do not assume a request has been approved.
- Do not invent leave balances or company policies.
- Do not change official leave records without authorization.
- Protect employee personal information.
- Use synthetic data for public examples and testing.
