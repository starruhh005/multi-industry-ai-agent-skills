# HR Document Organizer

## Purpose

Assist HR staff in classifying and organizing personnel documents so that employee records can be reviewed and retrieved more efficiently.

## When to Use

Use this skill when:
- Organizing employee personnel files.
- Classifying employment contracts and administrative documents.
- Reviewing document names and categories.
- Preparing document inventories.
- Identifying documents that may be missing from an employee file.

## Required Input

When available, use:
- Document files or document text.
- Employee reference or authorized employee code.
- Document type.
- Document date.
- Department or work unit.
- Company's document classification rules.

## Procedure

1. Review the documents provided by authorized HR staff.
2. Identify the document type based on its content and metadata.
3. Assign an appropriate category according to company classification rules.
4. Suggest a consistent document naming format.
5. Organize the document inventory by employee reference and document category.
6. Identify unreadable, incomplete, or potentially duplicated documents.
7. Prepare a structured document inventory.

## Suggested Document Categories

- Employee identity and personal administration.
- Employment contracts.
- Recruitment documents.
- Attendance and leave administration.
- Training and development.
- Employment letters.
- Other HR administrative documents.

## Output Format

Provide:
- Employee reference.
- Document name.
- Document category.
- Document date, if available.
- Document status.
- Missing or unreadable information.
- Notes requiring HR verification.

## Rules and Limitations

- Do not delete, move, or modify official files without authorization.
- Do not expose confidential personnel documents.
- Do not assume a document is valid merely because it is present.
- Do not invent document dates or employee information.
- Follow the company's document retention and classification policies.
- Use synthetic documents in public demonstrations.
