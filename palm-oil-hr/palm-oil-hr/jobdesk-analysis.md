# Jobdesk Analysis — Palm Oil Company HR Department

## 1. Department Overview

The Human Resources Department (HRD) manages employee-related administration and supports the company's workforce operations. In a palm oil company, HR administration may involve employees working in offices, plantations, processing facilities, and other operational areas.

Many HR activities involve repetitive data entry, document checking, record organization, and report preparation. These activities can be supported by AI Agents to improve administrative efficiency.

## 2. Jobdesk and Repetitive Activities

| No. | HRD Jobdesk | Repetitive Activity | Potential AI Agent Support |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Employee data administration | Entering and checking employee identity, position, department, and employment status | Validate data completeness and identify inconsistent entries |
| 2 | Attendance administration | Collecting daily attendance and calculating attendance summaries | Prepare attendance recaps and flag unusual records |
| 3 | Recruitment administration | Checking CVs and required applicant documents | Review document completeness and summarize applicant information |
| 4 | Personnel document management | Sorting employee contracts, identity documents, and other personnel files | Classify and organize documents |
| 5 | Leave and permission administration | Recording leave requests and calculating leave summaries | Prepare leave records and identify missing information |
| 6 | HR correspondence | Preparing employment, assignment, and administrative letters | Generate standardized letter drafts |
| 7 | Employee information services | Answering recurring questions about leave, attendance, and company procedures | Provide answers based on approved HR policies |
| 8 | HR reporting | Collecting employee, attendance, and administrative data for monthly reports | Prepare structured monthly HR reports |

## 3. Proposed AI Agent Skills

1. `employee-data-validator` — checks employee data completeness and consistency.
2. `attendance-recap-agent` — summarizes attendance records and identifies records requiring verification.
3. `recruitment-document-screener` — checks applicant document completeness and summarizes submitted information.
4. `hr-document-organizer` — classifies and organizes personnel documents.
5. `leave-permission-assistant` — helps record and summarize leave and permission requests.
6. `hr-letter-drafter` — prepares drafts of routine HR letters.
7. `employee-faq-assistant` — answers routine employee questions using approved company policies.
8. `hr-monthly-report-generator` — prepares monthly administrative HR reports.

## 4. Expected Benefits

- Reduces repetitive data entry and document checking.
- Makes HR records easier to organize and review.
- Helps HR staff prepare routine reports more efficiently.
- Improves consistency in administrative documents.
- Helps employees access routine HR information.
- Allows HR staff to focus more on tasks requiring communication, judgment, and decision-making.

## 5. Important Limitations

AI Agent support does not replace the authority of HR personnel. Recruitment decisions, disciplinary actions, employment termination, salary decisions, promotions, and final leave approvals must remain under authorized human control.

Employee information is confidential. Testing and public demonstrations must use fictional or synthetic data.
