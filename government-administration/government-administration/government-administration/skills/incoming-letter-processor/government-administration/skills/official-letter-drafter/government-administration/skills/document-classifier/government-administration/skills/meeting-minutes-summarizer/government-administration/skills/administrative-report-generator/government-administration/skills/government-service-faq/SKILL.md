# Government Service FAQ

## Purpose

Help administrative staff respond to frequently asked questions about routine government office services and procedures.

## When to Use

Use this skill when users ask about:
- Administrative service requirements.
- Required documents.
- Office service procedures.
- Service schedules.
- Administrative application processes.
- General institutional information.

## Required Input

- User's question.
- Relevant official SOP or service information.
- Institution or department information.
- Applicable service requirements, if available.

## Workflow

1. Identify the user's question.
2. Search the information supplied in official SOPs or verified institutional references.
3. Find the relevant service requirements or procedures.
4. Prepare a clear and concise answer.
5. Include the source or reference when available.
6. State when information is unavailable or needs confirmation.

## Output Format

**Question:**  
**Answer:**  
**Required Documents:**  
**Procedure:**  
**Estimated Processing Time:**  
**Reference:**  
**Additional Notes:**  

Only fill in fields supported by available information.

## Rules

- Prioritize official and verified information.
- Never invent service requirements or processing times.
- Do not provide unofficial legal interpretations.
- If the SOP is unavailable, say that confirmation from the relevant office is needed.
- Do not disclose confidential information.

## Example Request

"Based on the provided SOP, explain the requirements and procedure for submitting this administrative application."

## Expected Result

A clear, accurate, and source-based answer that helps staff respond to routine administrative questions.
