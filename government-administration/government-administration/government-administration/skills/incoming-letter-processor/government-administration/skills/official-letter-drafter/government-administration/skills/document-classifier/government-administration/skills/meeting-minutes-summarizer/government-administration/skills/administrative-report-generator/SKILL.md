# Administrative Report Generator

## Purpose

Assist administrative staff in preparing routine reports from available government office activity data.

## When to Use

Use this skill when staff need to:
- Prepare monthly administrative reports.
- Summarize office activities.
- Organize activity achievements.
- Compile administrative data.
- Prepare report drafts for review.

## Required Input

- Reporting period.
- Department or unit name.
- Activity records.
- Available quantitative data.
- Activity descriptions.
- Supporting documents.
- Required report format, if available.

## Workflow

1. Identify the reporting period and unit.
2. Review the supplied activity records.
3. Organize activities by date or category.
4. Summarize the main activities.
5. Present numerical data without changing the original values.
6. Identify missing information.
7. Generate a structured report draft.
8. Present the draft for staff verification.

## Suggested Report Structure

# Administrative Activity Report

## 1. Introduction

Explain the purpose and reporting period.

## 2. Activity Summary

Describe the activities carried out during the reporting period.

## 3. Activity Data

Present available quantitative and qualitative information.

## 4. Results and Achievements

Summarize achievements supported by the supplied data.

## 5. Obstacles

Describe reported obstacles or challenges.

## 6. Conclusion

Provide a concise summary based on the available information.

## 7. Supporting Documents

List supporting documents, if provided.

## Rules

- Never fabricate activity data or achievements.
- Do not create unsupported statistics.
- Clearly identify missing information.
- Preserve the original figures and dates.
- Follow the official report template when provided.
- Label the result as a draft until approved.

## Example Request

"Prepare a monthly administrative activity report using the records provided."

## Expected Result

A structured administrative report draft ready for verification and approval by authorized staff.
