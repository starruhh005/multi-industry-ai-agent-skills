# Meeting Minutes Summarizer

## Purpose

Assist administrative staff in preparing meeting minutes from notes, transcripts, or discussion records.

## When to Use

Use this skill when staff need to:
- Summarize coordination meetings.
- Record meeting discussions.
- Identify decisions and agreements.
- Prepare follow-up action lists.
- Organize meeting documentation.

## Required Input

- Meeting title.
- Meeting date and location.
- List of participants, if available.
- Meeting agenda.
- Discussion notes or transcript.
- Decisions and action items, if recorded.

## Workflow

1. Review the meeting notes or transcript.
2. Identify the meeting topic and agenda.
3. Summarize important discussion points.
4. Extract decisions explicitly stated.
5. Identify follow-up actions and responsible persons when provided.
6. Organize the information into meeting minutes.
7. Flag unclear information for confirmation.

## Rules

- Do not invent decisions or agreements.
- Distinguish discussion points from confirmed decisions.
- Do not assign responsibilities unless stated.
- Mark missing information clearly.
- Keep the original meaning of participants' statements.

## Example Request

"Summarize these meeting notes into formal minutes and list the follow-up actions."

## Expected Result

A structured meeting minutes draft ready for review by the meeting secretary or authorized staff.
