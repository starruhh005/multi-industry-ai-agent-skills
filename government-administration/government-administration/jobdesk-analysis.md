# Jobdesk Analysis — Government Administration

## 1. Overview

This document identifies administrative responsibilities and repetitive activities in a local government office that can be supported by an AI Agent.

The objective is to identify tasks that can be assisted or partially automated without replacing the authority and responsibilities of government employees.

## 2. Jobdesk and Repetitive Activities

| No. | Jobdesk | Repetitive Activity | AI Agent Support |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Incoming correspondence administration | Recording letter numbers, dates, senders, and subjects | Extracts and organizes incoming letter information |
| 2 | Outgoing correspondence administration | Preparing standardized official letters | Generates letter drafts based on approved templates |
| 3 | Document archiving | Grouping documents by type, date, and subject | Classifies documents and suggests archive categories |
| 4 | Meeting administration | Recording discussions and meeting decisions | Summarizes meeting notes and action items |
| 5 | Administrative reporting | Collecting and summarizing routine activity data | Generates structured report drafts |
| 6 | Public service information | Answering frequently asked administrative questions | Provides answers based on available official SOPs |

## 3. Tasks Suitable for AI Assistance

The following tasks are suitable for AI support:

- Extracting information from incoming letters.
- Preparing draft official correspondence.
- Categorizing administrative documents.
- Summarizing meeting notes.
- Preparing routine administrative report drafts.
- Answering frequently asked questions using official information.

## 4. Tasks Requiring Human Supervision

The following activities must not be performed independently by the AI Agent:

- Signing or approving official letters.
- Making government policy decisions.
- Issuing official dispositions.
- Confirming the legal validity of documents.
- Releasing confidential information.
- Approving official administrative reports.

## 5. Expected Benefits

1. Faster administrative document processing.
2. More organized document records.
3. Reduced repetitive manual work.
4. More consistent document formatting.
5. Improved efficiency in preparing routine reports.

## 6. Conclusion

AI Agents can support local government administration by assisting with repetitive documentation, correspondence, archiving, meeting summaries, and routine reporting.

However, human employees remain responsible for verification, approval, and all official decisions.
